      Game stats

      Hint

      Each hint costs $50 of your timer bonus.

      How to play

      The table is dealt 8 columns of 7 cards: a full deck plus four Jokers. Find poker hands made of cards that touch each other (left, right, above or below), select them, and play them. They're removed, you score their value, and the cards above them fall down. When a whole column empties, the columns to its right slide over to close the gap.

      Making a play

      • Click (or tap) cards to select them. Click again to deselect.
      • Press Play hand, right-click the table, or press Enter to play.
      • The bar above the hand list tells you what your selection makes before you play it.

      Jokers

      Jokers aren't wild. A Joker can remove one card it touches, taking itself away too. A Joker sitting on the bottom row is more powerful: it can remove any card on the table. Joker plays score nothing, but they're great for clearing a card that's in the way.

      Scoring

      • Every game costs a $1,000 entry fee from your bankroll. Once your hands are worth more than that, your score turns green.
      • The game ends when the computer finds no plays left. Your final score is your hands plus bonuses, and it's added to your bankroll.
      • Different hands: $250 for each type of hand you made at least once. The hand list lights up as you collect them.
      • Cards removed: $50 per card, plus a big bonus if you finish with 10 or fewer cards left.
      • Timer bonus: starts at $2,000 and drops $5 a second from your first play. Hints cost $50 of it.

      Tips

      • Go for the high-value hands first. You rarely beat the entry fee on pairs alone.
      • Work Jokers down to the bottom row, where they can remove anything.
      • Keep the columns even. A short column between two tall ones is worth clearing: the tall ones then slide together and create new hands.
      • Think a few moves ahead. Clearing one card can drop a whole column into a four of a kind.
